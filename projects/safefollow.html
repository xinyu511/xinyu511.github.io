SafeFollow: a robot that follows a person and avoids obstacles

An assistive robot that follows an older adult. It has to keep the right person in view, keep its distance, and stop in time.

The system

  • Vision (my part): YOLO11n running on the Pi's CPU, restricted to people, with ByteTrack for tracking. A stable-identity layer re-matches the target by position and a color histogram of the torso, so the robot keeps the same person as its target when tracker IDs change.
  • Distance: stereo depth from the eYs3D camera with OpenCV block matching, falling back to an estimate from the bounding-box size.
  • Control: a PID loop holding about 1.25 m, three ultrasonic sensors with caution and stop thresholds, and a four-state machine: follow, avoid, search, stop. My partner built the drive base and motor control.

Testing

Four of seven runs stayed inside the target band; the others ended at 1.55 m and 1.65 m (too far) and 0.85 m (too close). Detection held mAP@0.5 of 94.2% across six scenarios of 100 labeled images each, and the robot reacted to obstacles in 109 ms on average against a 200 ms requirement. Following worked at walking speeds of 0.2–0.5 m/s and failed at 0.6 m/s and above.
