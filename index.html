Why role-play LLMs drift in long conversations

Over 40+ turns, persona-conditioned LLMs stop sounding like their character. We found the failure looks like imitation, not forgetting: the model copies its own and its partner's earlier lines while still answering persona facts correctly. I built the harness: KV-cache-reusing MLX and Hugging Face backends, decoding-time interventions, and a blind LLM judge calibrated with planted errors.

  • ~225dialogues generated
  • 24/24persona facts still correct at turn 39
