Why role-play LLMs drift in long conversations

Over 40+ turns, persona-conditioned LLMs stop sounding like their character. We found the failure looks like imitation, not forgetting: the model copies its own and its partner's earlier lines while still answering persona facts correctly. I built the harness: KV-cache-reusing MLX/HF backends, decoding-time interventions, and a blind LLM judge calibrated with planted errors.

  • ~225dialogues generated
  • ~500blind-judged chunks
  • 24/24persona facts still correct at turn 39

Fall 2023

Question answering and generation over long documents

A two-person NLP course project. To answer a question about a long article, TF-IDF retrieval picks the relevant passages, a BERT classifier routes yes/no versus open questions, and fine-tuned T5 models answer each type. To generate questions, YAKE keywords become answers that a fine-tuned T5 writes questions for. Few-shot Llama-2 was tried and dropped as too slow and hard to control.

  • 0.811F1 on yes/no questions (BoolQ)
  • 0.763F1 on span answers (SQuAD)
  • 79%of sampled generated questions made sense

Measuring who vector search fails

Approximate nearest-neighbor search in pgvector returns good results for some groups of queries and poor results for others. I built the lab's offline measurement pipeline on ann-benchmarks: pgvector HNSW in Docker, swept over index settings, recording recall@10 per query and comparing recall across KMeans clusters of the query space. I added new workloads (GloVe, CIFAR embeddings, FairFace, DBpedia), co-designed the lab's adaptive loop that retunes search per query cluster at runtime, and helped build its monitor. Advised by Prof. Akshitha Sriraman.

  • 56%of GloVe-100 queries below 0.9 recall@10 at ef_search 10
  • 10kqueries per sweep, 8 search settings
